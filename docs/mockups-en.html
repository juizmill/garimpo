Garimpo

One window that answers one question: what is making the most gold right now? The addon reads Auction House prices (from Auctionator's scans), compares them with the last few days and ranks item categories by estimated profit.

Below are the eleven proposed screens. Values are examples, and the icons are simplified drawings: the addon shows the game's original icons.

1 · Ranking

Main screen, opened with /garimpo or the minimap icon.

  • Est. profit combines average price with how many items actually sell per day.
  • g/h is a farming estimate, calibrated from what you gathered in earlier sessions.
  • 24h shows whether the category's prices are rising or falling.
  • Clicking a row opens the category detail.

3 · Bags

The keeping side of gold: what you already carry and what to do with each item.

  • Adds up the market value of everything in your bags (and bank, once visited).
  • Separates what is worth selling now from what is worth keeping.
  • List on AH sends the marked items to Auctionator.

4 · Chart

Accumulated gold over time. This example works: change the period and hover over the line.

  • Each point is how much gold you had that day.
  • A rising line means you are saving gold. A drop shows a day of spending.
  • At the top: what you had at the start of the period, what you have now and the difference.
  • Data is saved in the addon itself, per character.

5 · At the Auction House

When you open the auctioneer, this panel docks beside the Auction House window.

  • It lists only items marked Sell, with the suggested price filled in.
  • Post on each row, or Post next to work down the list. Try the buttons in the example.
  • The game requires one click per post, so the addon can't post everything at once.
  • Markup: how far above the current AH price you want to sell. 0% is the current price, 100% is double (1g becomes 2g). Negative values sell cheaper.
  • Each item starts with its category's markup, set in Settings. Change the number in the example and watch the price update.

6 · Settings

The default values used by the Auction House panel.

  • Default markup applies to every item. 0% is the current AH price.
  • Markup by category replaces the default for that category only. In this example, Herbs post 10% higher and Transmog 25% higher.
  • Changing a value here updates the panel on screen 5, except rows you already edited by hand.

7 · Tooltip and compact bar

For players who don't want to open any window.

  • Hovering any item adds price, average, sales and the suggested action to its tooltip.
  • The bar shows current gold, session rate and the top category right now.

8 · Primary professions

Step by step from your current skill to the max, with each step's cost at today's prices.

  • One button per primary profession, same as the secondary ones. The example works.
  • The Leveling now box shows the current recipe, how many crafts are left and what comes next.
  • When there is an alternative recipe, Garimpo compares both at AH prices and marks the cheapest.
  • It tells you when to visit the trainer, with name, city and required level.
  • On Forever recipes turn grey 25 points earlier than in Classic, so the route is specific to Forever.
  • Mining shows where to mine at each range and tells you when it unlocks something for Blacksmithing.

9 · Buy or farm

The shopping list up to the next trainer, minus what is already in your bags and bank.

  • Type how much gold you want to spend. Garimpo first buys what saves the most farming time per gold and sends you to farm the rest.
  • Vendor items (the fluxes) are always bought.
  • Farm everything and Buy everything show the two extremes. Try the field in the example.
  • Search the AH opens an Auctionator search with the items marked to buy.

10 · Where to farm

Opens when you click a reagent on screen 9.

  • Compares three paths: buy it ready, buy the raw material and craft it, or farm it.
  • Here, buying ore and smelting costs half as much as buying Bronze Bars. The plan on screen 9 already uses that price.
  • Where to farm lists zones with level, ore and nodes per hour. The best zone for your faction comes first.
  • Mark on map places a waypoint on the game map.

11 · Secondary professions

First Aid, Cooking and Fishing don't take a profession slot, so every character can level all three.

  • Switch between the three at the top of the window. The example works.
  • Cooking picks the cheapest recipe for each range and uses what you fish. With Fishing, the cost drops from 31g to 19g.
  • Fishing has no reagents. It shows where to fish, the lure, the book and the quest for each range, and what the fish you catch are worth.
  • First Aid uses cloth, so it goes into the same buy-or-farm calculation as screen 9.
Data: Garimpo uses the Auction House scans Auctionator already stores, with no scanner of its own. The 7-day history and g/h are saved per character in the addon's SavedVariables. Profession routes are inspired by the wow-professions.com guides, credited in the addon.
